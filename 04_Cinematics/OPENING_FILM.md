# Opening film — 10–15 minute target

**Narrative coverage:** SIGNAL's polished but oppressive city; identity checks; mother and child denied medical treatment; 404 discovering classified material; erasure; prisoner transport; wilderness crash; drone pursuit and Mara's contact.

## Proposed scene order (edit plan, not final timecodes)
1. Central City establishing shots and broadcast.
2. Civilian district and surveillance.
3. Registration / medical authorization denial.
4. Tower and facility transition.
5. Architect workstation, Project Ghost and CROWN files.
6. Facility lockdown and identity deletion.
7. Prisoner processing / transport.
8. Mountain road and transport incident.
9. Crash-site awakening, drones in tree line, first Mara transmission.
10. End on 404 fleeing into the Dead Zone / playable transition.

The 18-location opening-film list in `LOCATION_TAXONOMIES.md` is a separate plan from the 15-image reference sheet.

## Video reference constraints
- Character images: one full-body character per image, plain white or transparent background, no props/text/background.
- Environment plates: no people or UI; consistent landmarks, weather, lighting, and architecture; 16:9 target.
- Minimax: maintain consistent character appearance and costume per shot.
- ComfyUI: source images are visual references, not true 4K originals; upscaling cannot restore source detail.
- Production: define shot IDs, camera, lens, duration, action, location ID, character IDs, audio and transitions before generating shots.
