# Campaign spine (working outline)

I. **The Error:** 404 escapes; restores abandoned relay; meets Haven; converts first tower; makes first public transmission.
II. **The Awakening:** Aurelia joins; Project Ghost comes into focus; Republic grows.
III. **The Frequency War:** Republic expansion, difficult political choices, major assault; Node Zero lost as headquarters.
IV. **Ghost Protocol:** 404's past and the CROWN system revealed; the Place Between remains unresolved.
V. **The New Republic:** Confront Rayne; decide the future of the network and the Republic.

The prior planning describes 31 main chapters overall, but detailed mission-by-mission source material available in this handoff covers Act I only. Do not fabricate remaining mission IDs or scripts.
