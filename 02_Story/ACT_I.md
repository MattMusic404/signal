# Act I — THE ERROR (production draft)

**Estimated gameplay:** 3–4 hours. **Six main missions; 21 cinematic beats**. Scene timing and dialogue are first-pass proposals, not final shooting scripts.

## CIN_001–003 / MAIN_001
001 **A Perfect Society**: SIGNAL city government broadcast. Mother with sick child denied medical authorization and escorted away. Theme: 'Freedom is a system permission.'
002 **Identity Termination**: Architect accesses Project Ghost/CROWN documents; disclosure detected; emergency identity deletion and blackout.
003 **The Wreck**: 404 awakens in overturned prisoner transport in wet forest. Wrist reads `DEV_404 / IDENTITY NOT FOUND`. Mara guides him; camera transitions behind his shoulder into gameplay.
Gameplay: crash site → fallen pines (drone searchlights) → broken bridge (pursuit) → emergency shelter (Signal Scan and access-panel puzzle). Optional `INTEL_001` transport manifest (`OMEGA` transfer).

## CIN_004–006 / MAIN_002
004 **The Coordinates**: Mara identifies herself and sends 404 toward an abandoned relay.
005 **The Forgotten Relay**: 404 recognizes an old independent network symbol; brief flash of architect memory.
006 **The First Connection**: old developer recording says a network should connect people by choice; brief unexplained purple butterfly; relay emits first blue pulse.
Gameplay: forest exploration, ranger station, monitoring post, beacon; restore generator, processor, antenna; introductory signal-routing puzzle.

## CIN_007–009 / MAIN_003
007 **The Supply Cache**: need regulator, power cell, components; SIGNAL soldiers at depot.
008 **Recovery Priority Omega**: terminal reveals `DEV_404 / ARCHITECT CLASS / RECOVER ALIVE / DIRECTOR RAYNE`.
009 **The Hunter**: Kade reviews 404's drone-disruption footage; orders recovery teams.
Gameplay: first firearm/cover encounter; Pulse Shock on Recon Drone; disable alarm optionally; retrieve components and escape.

## CIN_010–013 / MAIN_004
010 **A Call for Help**: Haven medical generator failing.
011 **Meeting Elias Rook**: wary settlement leader allows 404 to help.
012 **The Choice**: scarce regulator goes first to Haven medical power or Node Zero defenses. Save `MAIN_004_CHOICE`; allow later repair of neglected system. Consequences depend on preparation and follow-up.
013 **Node Zero**: Elias agrees to help; workshop, power, basic research, network overview unlock.
Gameplay: settlement dialogue, resource gathering, repairs, first constrained construction.

## CIN_014–017 / MAIN_005
014 **A Dangerous Idea**: 404 proposes rewriting a SIGNAL tower's routing instructions rather than destroying it.
015 **The Tower's Memory**: legacy Architect signature recognized; Project Ghost/Convergence hints; violet butterfly and ambiguous woman's voice.
016 **The First Liberated Tower**: red tower lights turn blue; independent valley broadcast coverage.
017 **SIGNAL Responds**: Kade orders a recovery assault.
Gameplay: maintenance tunnel, service road, ridge climb, or optional engineer-intel route; security uplink, power routing, Node Zero connection, defense during conversion.

## CIN_018–021 / MAIN_006
018 **The Warning**: Kade's recovery force approaches; prepare defenses and evacuation.
019 **The First Battle**: waves of Sentries, Recon Drones, Breachers; elite recovery officer miniboss; Kade remains remote.
020 **The Broadcast**: 404 publicly declares an independent Republic network; 'And this is our first Signal.'
021 **The Director**: Rayne sees Architect signature; orders Dr. Syn contacted; Project Ghost reactivated.
Gameplay: preparation, civilian-vs-network emergency power allocation, multi-phase base defense, restore transmission. End Act I; allow remaining Dead Zone content before Act II.

## Optional quests
- `SQ_I_001` Missing Caravan: rescue survivors / secure medical supplies; impacts defenders and medical support.
- `SQ_I_002` Voices of the Forgotten: recover three citizen recordings; unlock broadcast dialogue.
- `SQ_I_003` The Old Engineer: repair beacon and recruit technician; unlock alternate tower access.

## Narrative invariants
404 always escapes, Node Zero is established, first tower is converted, Republic makes its first broadcast. Choices alter trust, preparedness, losses, dialogue, and later consequences without blocking the main campaign.
